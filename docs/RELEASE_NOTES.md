## What's New in v0.1.0

### 1. Quantum State Simulation & Teleportation Engine
- Faithful simulation of EPR Bell pairs ($|\Phi^+\rangle$, $|\Phi^-\rangle$, $|\Psi^+\rangle$, $|\Psi^-\rangle$).
- Teleportation protocol with Bell-State Measurement (BSM) and Pauli correction operations ($I, X, Z, XZ$).
- Projective Pauli measurements in $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ bases with shot-based binomial sampling.
- Quantum noise channels modeling depolarizing noise, bit-flips, and phase-flips.

### 2. Comprehensive Multi-Layer Threat Detection (Zero AI/ML)
- **Classical & Quantum Forgery Detection**: Message digest binding + projective state tomography.
- **Signer & Verifier Impersonation Detection**: Identity registry, role-based authorization, and permission enforcement.
- **Replay Protection**: Strict sliding nonce cache, session tracking, and clock skew guards (100% rejection in test corpus).
- **Quantum Channel Manipulation**: QBER analytics, basis asymmetry detection, Total Variation Distance (TVD), and Wald's Sequential Probability Ratio Test (SPRT).

### 3. Explainable Verdicts & Immutable Audit Ledger
- Deterministic verdicts: `ACCEPT`, `REJECT`, `ESCALATE`.
- Structured reason codes and failure breakdowns.
- SHA-256 hash-chained immutable audit ledger providing tamper evidence.

### 4. Controlled Self-Healing & Cryptographic Rollback
- Incident-driven policy amendment proposals.
- Automated shadow regression testing against golden corpora.
- Cryptographic Ed25519 digital signature approval by administrators (no auto-deployment).
- Instant, 100% reversible policy rollback checkpoints.

### 5. Interactive Dashboard & Benchmark Suite
- Dark-mode web console for operators demonstrating all four attack pathways and self-healing.
- Reproducible benchmark runner evaluating 30 independent seeds across Corpora H, F, I, R, C, E.
